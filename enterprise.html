SonicSeal SDK for labels, studios and DJ-software vendors

The Mac App Store version of SonicSeal is a paid desktop application for individual producers and small labels (€79 one-time purchase). This page describes a SEPARATE commercial offering — a B2B integration license for record labels, mastering studios, promo-distribution platforms and DJ-software vendors (Pioneer, Native Instruments, Serato, etc.) who want to embed SonicSeal's forensic watermarking pipeline into their own products under a private licensing agreement.

What this is NOT

This is not an in-app purchase, not a subscription, not an upgrade path, not a tier of the consumer app. The Mac App Store version cannot trigger or unlock this. It is a commercial agreement between SonicSeal, S.L. and another company, negotiated under NDA and signed as a separate contract.

Who needs this

  • Record labels shipping pre-release promos to hundreds of DJs each month and wanting forensic traceback on every leak.
  • Mastering studios offering watermarked client deliveries as a value-added service.
  • DJ software vendors (Pioneer, Native Instruments, Serato, VirtualDJ) implementing the mutual-handshake protocol so SonicSeal-protected files only play in legitimate, attested builds.
  • Promo distribution platforms (Promo.ly, Inflyte, label-internal portals) integrating per-recipient watermarking into their existing upload flow.
