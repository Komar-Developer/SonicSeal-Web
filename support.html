Before you email — common fixes

Activation block won't activate (Direct License Edition)

  • Make sure you copied the entire block, including the -----BEGIN SONICSEAL LICENSE----- and -----END SONICSEAL LICENSE----- lines.
  • Paste it into the multi-line text area (not the single-line search bar).
  • If your email client added line breaks inside the base64 signature, that's fine — SonicSeal tolerates whitespace.
  • Confirm your Mac's date/time is correct (the signature includes an "issued at" timestamp that must be in the past).
  • The activation block is bound to 1 Mac per Pro license. If you need to move it to a new Mac, email us and we'll re-issue it.

Mac App Store purchase: nothing to activate

  • The Mac App Store edition is a paid-up-front app. There is no activation step — once it's downloaded from the App Store, all Pro features are immediately available.
  • If features appear locked after install, force-quit and relaunch the app. Apple's receipt is verified locally by macOS on launch.

Audio Player shows 0:00

  • Fixed in v1.0.7. If you're on an older build, update via Help → Check for Updates (Direct License Edition) or via the Mac App Store.

App won't open / "damaged" warning (Direct License Edition)

  • Right-click the app → Open → Open. macOS Gatekeeper sometimes flags newly notarized builds on first launch.
  • If the warning persists, run in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SonicSeal.app

Where is my data stored?

  • Mac App Store edition: ~/Library/Containers/com.sonicseal.app/Data/Library/Application Support/
  • Direct License Edition (DMG): ~/Library/Application Support/SonicSeal/

Response time

Mac App Store users: best effort, usually within 48 hours. For App Store billing issues, go through Apple's Report a Problem portal.
Direct License Edition (Pro / Label / Studio / Vendor) holders: priority email support, usually within 24 hours.

What we can't help with

  • Refunds for Gumroad purchases older than 30 days (per Gumroad's policy).
  • Recovering audio that you've already shared publicly (SonicSeal is preventive — it embeds the watermark before distribution).
  • Mac App Store billing issues — those must go through Apple's Report a Problem portal.
